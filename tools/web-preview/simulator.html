Note: rendering here is a JS reimplementation of the firmware theme functions. Keep renderTheme() in this file in lock-step with Test_Button_DMX/themes.cpp, otherwise this preview will lie. Fire-flash themes use the 800 ms ON / 3200 ms OFF idle cycle; others render continuously. Each tower shows its two fixtures: the uplight (LaluceNatz, full RGBW) and the accumulator strips (RGB-only, capped at 75%, no white channel) — so the strips never show the white tint and sit dimmer than the uplight. Press & hold to fire opens the Confluence (central valve) and every tower's Fire CH4 valve; releasing plays the end-cue white flash on the uplights only. The fire button is a local preview — it does not POST to the device.
